4. What is being claimed, and what is not

  • The promise checked is narrow and specific: after fsync returns, the data survives a crash, and the structure is never left inconsistent - at any of 120 injected power-loss points plus 40 double faults.
  • The checker (tools/minifleet_checks.py) and the acceptance harness travel with the product and are compared byte for byte against their source, so the criteria cannot have been edited to fit the result.
  • Not claimed: concurrency (one process owns the image), directories, large files (four data blocks per file), or a real block device.
